The service

MotorLog is a vehicle record-keeping and telematics tool. During the open beta all plans are free; paid plans activate at public launch and beta users keep their data.

Your data, your responsibility

You own the records you create. Maintenance forecasts, diagnostic explanations, and AI-extracted data are informational aids, not professional mechanical advice — verify part numbers, torque specs, and safety-critical work against factory documentation.

Acceptable use

Track vehicles you own or administer with the knowledge of their drivers. Don't attempt to access other users' data or disrupt the service.

Beta disclaimer

The service is provided as-is during beta, without warranty. We take backups seriously, but export your data (Settings → Export) for anything you cannot afford to lose.
