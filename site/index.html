The Instrument Cluster For Your Ownership

Everything a meticulous owner tracks — in one place

Fuelly-grade fuel math, dealer-grade service records, and telematics that used to require a fleet contract.

Fuel & MPG Engine

Full/partial/reset fill logic done right. Per-tank MPG, rolling averages, price tracking, and a red-dot early warning when a tank drops below trend — often the first sign of a dragging brake or clogged filter.

Maintenance Forecasting

Intervals by miles and months, projected to real calendar dates from how you actually drive. Parts lists per service — oil spec, quantity, OEM part numbers — so the upcoming job is also the shopping list.

AI Receipt Scanning

Photograph any service or pump receipt. Vendor, date, odometer, line items, and part numbers are extracted and filed — your records build themselves. Works for insurance cards and registrations too.

True Cost Per Mile

Fuel + service + insurance + registration, normalized to $/mile per vehicle. The keep-it-or-sell-it number, computed continuously — compare every vehicle in your fleet on one screen.

OBD-II Diagnostics

Pair a Bluetooth dongle: live RPM, coolant, fuel level, and voltage. Read check-engine codes with AI diagnosis for your exact engine — severity, likely causes, drive-or-tow guidance — and clear them when fixed.

Fleet Telematics

A plug-in device logs every trip over WiFi + LTE — routes, engine data, check-engine alerts, and mileage that advances your maintenance countdowns automatically. Export an IRS-ready mileage log any time.
